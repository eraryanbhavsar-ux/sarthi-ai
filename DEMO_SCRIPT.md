# SARTHI — 3 to 5 Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **Product Name:** SARTHI  
> **Tagline:** Understand. Hear. Translate. Act.  
> **Theme:** AI for Accessibility & Inclusion  
> **Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges & Evaluators

---

## Demo Overview (Timing Breakdown)

- **0:00 – 0:20 (20 sec):** Problem Hook & 10-Second Value Proposition
- **0:20 – 1:00 (40 sec):** Upload & Immediate Structured Understanding
- **1:00 – 1:45 (45 sec):** The Killer Feature — "WHAT DO I NEED TO DO?" Checklist
- **1:45 – 2:30 (45 sec):** Plain Language & "Make It Even Simpler" Mode
- **2:30 – 3:15 (45 sec):** Multilingual Inclusion (Marathi/Hindi) & Voice Narration
- **3:15 – 3:50 (35 sec):** Step-by-Step Cognitive Mode & Grounded Q&A
- **3:50 – 4:30 (40 sec):** Universal Accessibility Toolbar & WCAG Sandbox
- **4:30 – 5:00 (30 sec):** Dashboard Persistence & Real-World Impact

---

## Minute-by-Minute Presentation Script

### 1. The Hook (0:00 – 0:20)
**Speaker:**
> *"Every single day, millions of people with disabilities, literacy barriers, or language differences receive critical notices like this government scholarship circular or hospital discharge summary. It is packed with dense legalese, confusing clauses, and hidden deadlines.*  
> *Generic chatbots explain what things mean, but they do not turn information into an accessible, actionable workflow.*  
> *Welcome to **SARTHI** — where our mission is: **Understand. Hear. Translate. Act.**"*

---

### 2. Upload & Immediate Understanding (0:20 – 1:00)
**Action:**
1. Navigate to the **Landing Page** and click **"Try SARTHI"** or **"Workspace"**.
2. Point out the command center inputs: PDF, Image, Text, and Voice.
3. Click the **"⚡ Try Sample (Scholarship Notice)"** button to simulate instant processing of a complex 3-page government scholarship circular.

**Speaker:**
> *"Let's test this complex National Higher Education Scholarship Notice. In one click, SARTHI's AI pipeline reads the content, filters out bureaucratic noise, and delivers an executive **Accessibility Summary**.*  
> *Notice right away: High difficulty, 5 required actions, 5 mandatory documents, and an urgent deadline of October 15th."*

---

### 3. The Killer Feature: "WHAT DO I NEED TO DO?" (1:00 – 1:45)
**Action:**
1. Click the **"Actions ('What To Do')"** tab.
2. Check off the first item: *"Get your Tahsildar Income Certificate (Form 16-B)"*.
3. Note how the progress bar advances from 0% to 20%.
4. Click the small audio icon next to an action step to hear it aloud.

**Speaker:**
> *"This is our major differentiator: **WHAT DO I NEED TO DO?**  
> Instead of leaving users overwhelmed with a 500-word paragraph, SARTHI automatically structures the notice into an interactive checklist.  
> Users can check off each requirement, hear instructions aloud, and track their progress with complete persistence."*

---

### 4. Cognitive Accessibility & "Make It Even Simpler" (1:45 – 2:30)
**Action:**
1. Switch to the **"Simple Mode"** tab.
2. Show the plain language summary (replacing *"furnish documentary evidence"* with *"provide documents that prove your details"*).
3. Click the **"✨ Make It Even Simpler"** button.
4. Show the maximum cognitive clarity card with the everyday train analogy and the 3 bite-sized takeaways.

**Speaker:**
> *"For individuals with cognitive disabilities, neurodivergence, or high reading fatigue, even simple summaries can be difficult.  
> When we click 'Make It Even Simpler', SARTHI distills the notice into short 10-word sentences, generates an everyday analogy, and provides three immediate takeaways."*

---

### 5. Multilingual Inclusion & Voice Mode (2:30 – 3:15)
**Action:**
1. Switch to the **"Translate"** tab.
2. Click **"मराठी (Marathi)"** or **"हिन्दी (Hindi)"**.
3. Point out that the explanation, key highlights, and action checklist are all localized.
4. Click **"Hear in MR"** or use the persistent **Audio Player Dock** at the bottom to adjust reading speed to 1.25x.

**Speaker:**
> *"In countries with diverse linguistic populations, official documents are rarely available in a person's mother tongue.  
> Here, SARTHI translates the entire workflow into natural Marathi. And with our built-in speech synthesis, users can hear the guidance read aloud with adjustable playback speeds."*

---

### 6. Step-by-Step Mode & Grounded Q&A (3:15 – 3:50)
**Action:**
1. Click the **"Step-by-Step"** tab to show the single-step focus card (*"Step 1 of 5: Check your eligibility"*).
2. Click **"Next Step"**.
3. Switch to the **"Ask SARTHI AI"** tab.
4. Click a suggested question: *"What is the maximum family income allowed?"*
5. Show the grounded answer (*"Under ₹3,50,000 per annum"*), verified strictly against the source.

**Speaker:**
> *"Step-by-step mode eliminates cognitive overload by showing only one instruction at a time in large typography.  
> And if the user has questions, 'Ask SARTHI AI' provides answers strictly grounded in the document — guaranteed with zero hallucinations."*

---

### 7. Universal Accessibility Toolbar (3:50 – 4:30)
**Action:**
1. Click the floating **"Accessibility Controls"** button in the bottom right corner.
2. Toggle **Dark Amber Contrast** (instant amber on pure deep black).
3. Toggle **Dyslexia Reading Mode** (letter and line spacing expand instantly).
4. Scale text to **X-Large (130%)**.
5. Close the toolbar to show the entire application adapting seamlessly.

**Speaker:**
> *"Accessibility is not an afterthought or a hidden settings page in SARTHI.  
> Our universal accessibility toolbar allows users to toggle dark high contrast, light contrast, dyslexia-friendly spacing, reduced motion, and large text scales across the entire application in real time."*

---

### 8. Persistence & Real-World Impact (4:30 – 5:00)
**Action:**
1. Navigate to the **Dashboard**.
2. Show the real user analytics: documents analyzed, actions completed, questions answered.
3. Show the saved session list with direct reopen and delete controls.

**Speaker:**
> *"All sessions and checklist states are securely persisted in MongoDB.  
> SARTHI does not treat disability as a limitation. Rather, it bridges the gap when digital systems fail to accommodate diverse human abilities.  
> SARTHI empowers every individual to Understand, Hear, Translate, and Act independently. Thank you!"*
